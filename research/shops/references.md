# Takanawa shop and station references

Checked online 2026-10-31. These are references for original pixel artwork, not claims of merchant participation. Downloaded photos retain their source copyrights; use them as working references, not as distributable game textures. No photo was edited.

## Station

**Takanawa Gateway Station.** [Architect's project page](https://kkaa.co.jp/en/project/takanawa-gateway-station/) confirms an origami-shaped steel and Japanese-cedar roof frame with white membrane. The photos credit East Japan Railway Company. These are circa-opening architecture photos, so use the station structure, not the historic plaza or surrounding construction, for current geography.

- `station-roof-kkaa.jpg`: https://kkaa.co.jp/img/2020/05/2.jpg (1200 × 800). Angled exterior. Long folded white eaves; warm timber underside; pale diagonal structural braces; broad blue-gray glass; two-story horizontal deck.
- `station-exterior-kkaa.jpg`: https://kkaa.co.jp/img/2020/05/1.jpg (1200 × 800). Frontal elevated exterior. Low, wide zigzag white roof; glass center; diagonal braces and large entrance stair. This is the best reference for the roof footprint.

## Three verified shops west of the station

1. **Matsushimaya / 松島屋**, Japanese sweets, **1-5-25 Takanawa, Minato-ku, Tokyo**. Independent traditional shop. [Toei transport's official local guide](https://www.kotsu.metro.tokyo.jp/pickup_information/magazine/fureai/web/spot/2024-08/) confirms address; [Sarai's May 4, 2026 report](https://serai.jp/gourmet/1261403) confirms ongoing business. A [dated August 2026 visit](https://www.karadabijin.world/?p=3696) documents the facade. `matsushimaya-facade-2026.jpg` is the unchanged original from https://www.karadabijin.world/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/matsushimaya-04-exterior.jpg (1600 × 1217), by the blog author Aki. Observed: alternating red/cream awning with scalloped edge, white noren with dark-red lettering, narrow bronze/metal frames, burgundy square tiles beneath the large left display window, recessed doorway on right, small potted greenery. **Strongest shop sprite reference.** Do not reproduce identifiable customers.

2. **Koizumi Gishidō / 義士堂 小泉商店**, souvenirs and sweets, **2-1-30 Takanawa, Minato-ku, Tokyo**. Independent shop beside Sengakuji's gate. [Official site](https://www.gishido.com/) has announcements dated October 28, 2026 and explicitly describes the shop, sweets, drums and Chushingura souvenirs. [Official access page](https://www.gishido.com/access.html) says about seven minutes from Takanawa Gateway, via the Route 15 crossing; it was fetched directly with curl when the web reader failed. The [official 2025 city guidebook](https://www.takanawagateway-city.com/assets/pdf/250324/guidebook_jp.pdf) confirms the address and temple-front setting; [Tokyo's March 2026 guide](https://www.gotokyo.org/book/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/AO_2603_minato-rurubu_low_EN.pdf) also lists the shop. No facade image saved, so do not invent a detailed branded storefront. Its public approach location is the closest fit for the station-to-temple route.

3. **Boulangerie Seiji Asakura / ブーランジェリー セイジアサクラ**, bakery, **2-6-20 Takanawa, Asahi Takanawa Mansion 104, Minato-ku, Tokyo**. Local independent bakery farther uphill, not immediately on the station-to-temple route. [Minato's September 2025 shopping association list](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/documents/64672/20250908143134.pdf) confirms address; [Takanawa Gateway City's March 31, 2026 notice](https://www.takanawagateway-city.com/news/detail.php?page=urn_ngsi-ld_EventInfo_9736) confirms the operating bakery's April pop-up. The pop-up was temporary, not its permanent location. [Bakery trade journal's facade report](https://www.boulansserie.com/article3618.html) describes its narrow, all-red frontage; photos there are historical. Use address/existence confidently, but treat exact facade condition as unverified for October 2026.

## Optional bakery photo, outside the close route

`liebe-facade-official.jpg` is the unchanged composite https://www.t-kaisei.co.jp/liebe/img/info_photo.jpg (480 × 209), on [Liebe's official site](https://www.t-kaisei.co.jp/liebe/). Address **3-9-7 Takanawa**. It combines a storefront photo with a staff portrait, so it is weaker than Matsushimaya for generation. The left portion shows brown brick piers, full-height glass, orange A-frame chalkboard and potted foliage. Official website currently lists the shop, but no dated 2026 operating notice was found. It lies farther south near Takanawadai, so omit it from a compact station-to-temple map.
